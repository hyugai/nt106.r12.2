# Lab 01 — Bài 03 Tasks

## Goal

Convert supported foreign currencies to VND.

Required rates:

```text
USD = 22,772 VND
EUR = 28,132 VND
GBP = 31,538 VND
SGD = 17,286 VND
JPY = 214 VND
```
